Frequently Asked Questions

How can AI help higher ed enrollment marketing?

AI can optimize your content for better visibility in search and AI platforms, automate repetitive marketing tasks, provide predictive analytics for enrollment trends, and help personalize communications at scale while maintaining ethical standards.

What can MarCom teams automate with AI?

Marketing and communications teams can automate content creation workflows, social media scheduling, analytics reporting, email campaigns, SEO optimization, data analysis, and routine content updates while maintaining brand voice and quality standards.

How do universities adopt AI responsibly?

Responsible AI adoption requires clear governance frameworks, FERPA compliance protocols, accessibility considerations, ethical guidelines, staff training, transparent communication about AI use, and continuous monitoring of AI outputs for bias and accuracy.

Is AI integration FERPA compliant?

AI can be FERPA compliant when implemented correctly. We help you establish data handling protocols, select appropriate tools, implement access controls, and create policies that protect student privacy while leveraging AI capabilities.
